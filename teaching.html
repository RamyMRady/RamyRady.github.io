ECEN 215

Principles of Electrical Engineering

Texas A&M University

Graduate Assistant Lecturer · 2019–2024

Fundamentals of electric circuit analysis and an introduction to electronics, for engineering majors outside electrical and computer engineering.

Format
3 credits: 2 hours of lecture and 2 hours of lab each week
Audience
Undergraduate engineering majors other than ECE
Prerequisites
MATH 251 or 253, and PHYS 207 or 208
Taught
Spring 2022 lectures; instructor of record in Spring 2024 (sections 501–503)
Textbook
Allan R. Hambley, Electrical Engineering: Principles and Applications, 7th ed.
