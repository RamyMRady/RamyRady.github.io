Tip · RF basics

Quick dB arithmetic you can do in your head

Ramy Rady · October 2026

Most link-budget mistakes are caught by a quick mental check. These ratios cover nearly every case:

dBPower ratioVoltage ratio
11.26×1.12×
32×1.41×
64×2×
1010×3.16×
20100×10×

Build anything else from these: 13 dB is 10 + 3, so 20× in power. 7 dB is 10 − 3, so 5×.

Pitfall

Know whether you're in power or voltage. A 6 dB gain doubles the voltage but quadruples the power. In optical links, a 1 dB change in optical power becomes a 2 dB change in RF power after the photodiode, because photocurrent is proportional to optical power and RF power goes as current squared.
