Sanity-check any receiver with −174 dBm/Hz

Ramy Rady · September 2026

At 290 K, thermal noise is −174 dBm in every hertz of bandwidth. Add 10·log10(B), the noise figure, and the SNR your demodulator needs, and you have the receiver's sensitivity.

Pmin ≈ −174 + 10·log10(B) + NF + SNRmin dBm

Useful bandwidth values to remember: 1 MHz is 60 dB, 20 MHz is 73 dB, 100 MHz is 80 dB, 1 GHz is 90 dB.

Tip

Use it to check a datasheet claim. If a 100 MHz receiver claims −100 dBm sensitivity with 10 dB SNR, the floor alone is −174 + 80 + 10 = −84 dBm before any noise figure. Reaching −100 dBm would need a noise figure of −16 dB, which is impossible. Something in the claim is off.
